OUR HERITAGE & FOUNDATION

Warehouse Racking Built Around Your Operation

ProRack builds on the warehouse storage experience of Silver Lining Storage Solutions. The business now brings racking design, manufacturing and installation together under ProRack Global.

We plan storage around the goods you handle, the space available and the way your team works. The aim is to improve storage capacity and access while meeting the project's structural requirements.

"We design every warehouse solution around the customer's operational requirements, storage profile, material-handling systems, available space, and future growth trajectory."

Our Core Operating Pillars
