OUR HERITAGE & FOUNDATION

Warehouse Racking Built Around Your Operation

ProRack Storage Solutions India Pvt. Ltd. is a warehouse engineering and storage solutions company within the ProRack AI Group. The business builds on the legacy of Silver Lining Storage Solutions, the warehouse storage business of Mangal Industries, part of the Amara Raja Group.

As we take ownership of this legacy, we are developing the business with a focus on accountability, transparency and AI integration across warehouse engineering and project delivery.

"We design every warehouse solution around the customer's operational requirements, storage profile, material-handling systems, available space, and future growth trajectory."

Niket Sherlekar

Group Chief Operating Officer, ProRack Global

Niket Sherlekar leads the Group’s operating agenda across ProRack Storage Solutions, SmartFit Solutions and the wider ProRack Global platform. His focus is on building the systems, teams and execution capability needed to deliver warehouse infrastructure at scale.

Before joining ProRack Global, Niket led infrastructure supply chain and expansion for Blinkit’s dark-store and warehouse network. His responsibilities included racking and storage solutions, material-handling and other capital assets, and MEP services.

He has led Category P&Ls, spent five years as a South Asia Segment Leader at DuPont, and brings seven years of entrepreneurial experience. He combines operating rigour with a practical builder’s mindset.

About ProRack

Company questions

Who owns and runs ProRack AI?

ProRack AI is the brand of ProRack Storage Solutions India Private Limited, a member company of the ProRack AI Group. The company is led by Managing Director Roshan Sudhir, with Niket Sherlekar as Group Chief Operating Officer, Girish Annath as Director, Kshitij Salve as VP, Sales and Marketing, and Prajakta Roshan as Administration Head.

What is the relationship between ProRack and Silver Lining Storage Solutions?

ProRack Storage Solutions builds on the legacy of Silver Lining Storage Solutions, the warehouse storage business of Mangal Industries, part of the Amara Raja Group.

Where is ProRack Storage Solutions registered?

The registered office is H.No.10-2-294/Plot No.177, Flat No.303, 3rd Floor, Royal Sai Mahalaxmi Pride, West Marredpally, Secunderabad 500026, Telangana, India. The company serves warehousing and manufacturing hubs across India and supports Middle East export projects.

Which ProRack businesses deliver warehouse projects?

ProRack Storage Solutions designs and manufactures racking, shelving, mezzanines and rack-clad structures. SmartFit Solutions handles surveying and site installation, and ProMec Integration covers fire safety, HVAC, electrical and material-handling infrastructure.
