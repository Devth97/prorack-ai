OPERATIONAL TRANSPARENCY

Structured Change Order Management

In complex industrial projects, warehouse floor plans and operational layouts frequently evolve. Uncontrolled changes create site delays, mismatched components, and structural risks. ProRack operates a formalized engineering change order framework:

1. Digital Revision Control

Every layout revision is version-controlled with automated DWG/IFC diff comparisons, ensuring site teams never install outdated drawings.

2. Tonnage & Coil Reallocation

Material changes immediately sync with shop floor coil nesting software to prevent scrap and maintain delivery schedules.

3. Complete Commercial Clarity

Clients receive clear, itemized impact summaries covering cost variations, structural implications, and handover dates prior to execution.

ENGINEERING FAQs

Common Technical Questions

What structural calculations are conducted before manufacturing begins?

ProRack performs full global and local finite element structural analysis: upright buckling capacity, beam end connector shear and moment rotation, baseplate bearing stresses on the concrete slab, anchorage pull-out resistance, and dynamic seismic drift under IS 1893 and EN 15512 specifications.

How does ProRack ensure material traceability on the production line?

Every coil of high-tensile steel is logged with mill heat test certificates (MTC), yield strength verification, and coil heat tracking. Components are barcoded through roll-forming, punching, welding, and powder coating, giving clients verifiable traceability for quality audits.
