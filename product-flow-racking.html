Pallet & Carton Flow Racking

Gravity-assisted stock movement for replenishment and picking workflows.

Discuss this system → Read the FAQs ↓

Overview

How it works

Flow storage uses sloped lanes and suitable rollers or tracks to advance loads toward the picking face. Its design must balance load size, speed control, safe separation and the required stock rotation pattern.

Typical applications

  • Perishable and batch-controlled stock
  • Carton order picking
  • High-frequency replenishment

FAQs

Common questions

What is pallet flow racking?

It is a high-density gravity-assisted storage system in which pallets move through appropriately designed lanes toward a discharge point.

How is carton flow different?

Carton flow is designed for smaller cartons or bins at picking faces; pallet flow is designed for full pallet loads and different handling demands.

Does flow racking support FIFO?

Correctly arranged loading and picking at opposite ends can support FIFO; actual stock rotation depends on the operating process.

How are loads controlled on sloped lanes?

Engineered brakes, separation devices and rollers are selected for the load properties and safety requirements.
