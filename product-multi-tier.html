Multi-Tier Shelving

Use vertical space for organised hand-picking and parts storage.

Discuss this system → Read the FAQs ↓

Overview

How it works

Multi-tier shelving introduces accessible picking levels within the warehouse. The arrangement brings together shelving, walkways, stairs, guardrails and material movement systems for the intended picking operation.

Typical applications

  • Automotive spare parts
  • E-commerce order picking
  • Small-parts and apparel operations

Number of levels
Subject to clear height, structural design and fire regulations.
Floor and shelf loading
Set according to stored goods and people movement.
Access

Decking

Goods transfer
Conveyors, lifts or handling provisions, if specified.

Technical specifications are design considerations, not fixed certified performance values. Confirm claims with engineering and supplier data before deployment.

FAQs

Common questions

What is multi-tier shelving?

It is an engineered arrangement with shelving and accessible working levels to use vertical space for smaller-item storage and picking.

Can people work safely on upper tiers?

Walkways, stairs, guardrails, loads, emergency exits and fire protection must be designed and approved for the intended occupancy and use.

Can conveyors connect the tiers?

Conveyors or lifting equipment can be considered when the operating flow and structural design support them.

How is the number of levels decided?

Clear height, headroom, floor loading, fire requirements and expected picking volumes govern the practical arrangement.
