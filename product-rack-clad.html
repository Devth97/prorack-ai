Rack-Clad Buildings

Storage systems engineered as part of the supporting building structure.

Overview

How it works

Rack-clad concepts integrate the storage rack structure with building enclosure and access requirements. The complete structural and operational system must be engineered together, from load paths to wind actions, fire strategy and installation sequencing.

Typical applications

  • High-bay storage facilities
  • Distribution warehouses
  • Integrated industrial storage projects

FAQs

Common questions

What is a rack-clad building?

It is a facility where storage racks also form part of the structural support for the building envelope, subject to specialised engineering.

How does it differ from a conventional warehouse?

In conventional buildings the primary building frame and racks are largely separate, whereas a rack-clad design integrates the structural roles.

What design approvals are essential?

Structural, wind, fire, foundation and local building requirements must be reviewed for the proposed site and configuration.

Can an automated handling system be integrated?

Potentially, when the project design coordinates equipment, rack structure, access, controls and maintenance requirements.
