Selective Pallet Racking

Direct access to individual pallet locations, designed for flexible everyday warehouse operations.

Discuss this system → Read the FAQs ↓

Overview

How it works

Selective pallet racking gives handling equipment direct access to pallet locations in dedicated bays. The engineering design responds to the pallet dimensions, unit loads, forklift envelope, operating environment and planned storage growth.

Typical applications

  • FMCG distribution
  • Mixed-SKU retail replenishment
  • Third-party logistics and spare parts

Engineering

Design considerations

Pallet dimensions
Defined by pallet width, depth, overhang and unit load.
Bay and beam loads
Specified through an approved structural design and load notice.
Aisle clearance
Based on the handling equipment manufacturer and turning geometry.
Storage levels
Set by clear height, lift reach and safety clearance.
Stock access
Direct pallet-level access; rotation policy managed operationally.

Technical specifications are design considerations, not fixed certified performance values. Confirm claims with engineering and supplier data before deployment.

FAQs

Common questions

What is selective pallet racking?

It is a rack system that provides direct forklift access to individual pallet positions from an aisle, making it suitable for mixed inventory.

How much weight can a rack bay carry?

Allowable loads depend on the approved upright, beam, bracing, connection, floor and anchorage design. Request the project-specific load chart rather than relying on a generic figure.

Can the layout be expanded later?

Modular layouts may permit extensions or beam adjustments, subject to a structural review, equipment compatibility and safe installation.

What details are needed for a quotation?

Share pallet dimensions and weights, required positions, clear height, handling equipment, site drawings, location and expected operating throughput.
