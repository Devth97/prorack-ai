Wide-Aisle Multi-Bay Racking

Conventional pallet storage with aisle space for efficient handling and flexible access.

Overview

How it works

Wide-aisle layouts combine pallet-rack bays with truck access routes. The design balances aisle width, vehicle movements, rack capacity and staging requirements to support straightforward operation.

Typical applications

  • General warehousing
  • Logistics distribution centres
  • Manufacturing components storage

Engineering

Design considerations

Aisle dimensions
Confirmed against the selected forklift or reach-truck specification.
Beam configuration
Designed to match unit loads and pallet dimensions.
Travel and staging space
Planned from one-way or two-way traffic and pedestrian safety.
Rack height
Subject to lifting equipment, roof height and fire requirements.
Flow strategy
Supports location-based picking and replenishment plans.

Technical specifications are design considerations, not fixed certified performance values. Confirm claims with engineering and supplier data before deployment.

FAQs

Common questions

When is wide-aisle racking suitable?

It is suitable when forklifts need conventional access and the operation values flexibility and simpler circulation over the highest possible storage density.

How wide should the aisle be?

Aisle width must be calculated for the chosen truck model, load overhang, turning envelope and operating conditions.

Can different pallet sizes share the same warehouse?

They can when locations, beams and handling equipment are engineered for the resulting pallet and load combinations.

How does wide-aisle differ from VNA?

VNA can reduce aisle area using specialist guided equipment, while wide-aisle generally accommodates more conventional truck movements.
