Wide-Aisle Multi-Bay Racking

Wide-aisle multi-bay racking is pallet racking laid out with aisles of about 3.0 m to 3.8 m so that standard counterbalance and reach forklifts can work and pass each other. ProRack manufactures it in Hyderabad for 3PL, distribution and general warehouses across India.

How it works

How wide-aisle multi-bay racking works

Long runs of pallet racking are arranged in multi-bay rows with aisles wide enough for forklifts to turn, pass and stage pallets. Several trucks can work at the same time, which suits high put-away and dispatch volumes.

Because no specialised trucks are needed, a wide-aisle layout works with an existing forklift fleet and is simple to operate and expand.

Is it the right fit?

When to choose wide-aisle multi-bay racking

  • Several forklifts work in the same area at peak times
  • You want to use your existing counterbalance or reach trucks
  • Fast put-away and dispatch matter more than maximum density
  • You need staging space next to the racking

When another system may fit better

  • Very narrow-aisle racking when you need more pallet positions on the same footprint.

  • Drive-in racking for bulk storage of a few SKUs.

Questions

Wide-Aisle Multi-Bay Racking FAQs

What is the difference between wide-aisle and very narrow-aisle racking?

Wide-aisle racking uses aisles of about 3.0 m to 3.8 m and standard counterbalance or reach forklifts. Very narrow-aisle racking reduces aisles to about 1.5 m and uses guided turret trucks or order pickers, giving higher storage density but requiring specialised equipment and a very flat floor.

Which forklifts can work in a wide-aisle racking layout?

Standard counterbalance forklifts and reach trucks. The exact aisle width is set by the truck model, load size and turning radius during layout design.

Who is wide-aisle racking best suited for?

It suits 3PL fulfilment centres, general warehousing and heavy machinery spares, where many forklift movements happen at the same time and throughput matters more than maximum density.
